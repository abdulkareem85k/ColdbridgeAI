The Challenge

Fresh produce is lost before it reaches the market

Structural gaps in first-mile rural cooling force perishable crops into rapid degradation and distress sales.

Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, factual answers regarding the platform mechanics, algorithms, and prototype scope.

What is ColdBridge AI?
ColdBridge AI is a decentralized platform connecting smallholder farmers with regional cold storage facilities and mobile cooling vans on a flexible, pay-per-use basis.
What is the Spoilage Risk Index?
The Spoilage Risk Index (SRI) is a mathematical score (0 to 100) combining crop-specific perishability, ambient heat exposure above physiological thresholds, and elapsed transit hours.
How does Smart Asset Matching work?
The matching algorithm scores regional assets using multi-criteria optimization: Price (30%), Time/ETA (20%), Reliability (20%), Distance (15%), and Capacity (15%), with an urgency bonus for high-risk batches.
What is reverse auction bidding?
Farmers list harvested produce batches with cooling parameters, and regional storage and transport operators submit competitive downward per-kg per-day bids to fill unutilized capacity.
How does the pay-per-use model work?
Farmers pay only for the exact kilogram volume and days of cooling required, eliminating the necessity of owning costly refrigerated hardware or signing monolithic fixed warehouse contracts.
Are all listed cold-storage facilities registered partners?
No. Public cold storage facilities from government directories are displayed for regional geographic awareness and are explicitly labelled "Not a ColdBridge partner". Only registered operators accept digital bookings.
Are the offers and solar telemetry real or simulated?
In this prototype demonstration, simulated solar telemetry and sample operator bids are utilized to demonstrate end-to-end algorithmic decision making.
How can a cold-storage operator join the platform?
Operators can register via the Sign Up portal, select the Cold Storage Operator role, configure chamber specifications and pricing, and start receiving verified farmer cooling bookings.
